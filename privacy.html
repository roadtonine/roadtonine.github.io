Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy covers the Road to 9 website (roadtonine.github.io) and Road to 9 Uploader, an internal tool the Road to 9 channel uses to publish its own videos to YouTube.

Road to 9 Uploader

Road to 9 Uploader is not a public app. It is used only by the owner and managers of the Road to 9 YouTube channel (@RoadToNine) to upload videos, captions, thumbnails and playlist entries to that channel.

  • YouTube API Services. The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • What it accesses. With your permission (Google OAuth), it uploads and updates videos, captions, thumbnails and playlists on the authorised channel, and reads that channel's own video and playlist list so it does not upload twice. It does not read comments, subscribers' details, or any other user's data.
  • What it stores. Only the OAuth access/refresh token, kept in a private, access-restricted location on the channel's own server. It is never shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • How to remove access. You can revoke the tool's access at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections (also security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). The stored token is then useless and is deleted within 30 days.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect any personal information. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).

Viewers of our videos

Road to 9 does not collect personal information from viewers. Watching our videos on YouTube is covered by YouTube's own terms and the Google Privacy Policy. Our audience is students aged 14–16 and above; the channel is not made for children under 13.
